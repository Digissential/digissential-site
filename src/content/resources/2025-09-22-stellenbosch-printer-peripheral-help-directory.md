## Common pitfalls (and the fix)

- **Wrong driver/paper width** → Install the exact model driver and match **57/80 mm** or A4 settings in OS *and* app.  
- **Guest SSID isolation** → Move printers to a **staff/private SSID**; keep guests isolated.  
- **DHCP roulette** → Reserve the printer’s IP to prevent mid-shift re-addressing.  
- **Mesh “smart connect”** → Pin legacy printers to **2.4 GHz** with modest roaming.  
- **SMTP changes** → Use **app passwords** with 2-step verification; verify ports/TLS and from-address policy.

## POPIA-aware scan-to-email (what we set)

- Use **authenticated SMTP** (Gmail/M365) with **TLS** and app passwords/MFA.  
- **Limit address books** and default destinations; avoid auto-email to personal accounts without consent.  
- Keep a simple **audit note**: device, address used, and when credentials change.  
- Prefer **local scan-to-folder** on a secured share for bulk scanning in SMEs.
