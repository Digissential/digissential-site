## Safe steps (do this now)

1. **Disconnect** from Wi-Fi/Ethernet if you see persistent pop-ups or credential prompts.  
2. **Update first**, then run **Microsoft Safety Scanner** (Windows) as a **full scan** from a fresh download. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}  
3. **Reset the browser** and remove unknown **extensions**; check startup items.  
4. **Stop DIY** if symptoms persist—book a professional clean to avoid data loss.  
5. **Back up** key folders; for retired devices, request a **Certified Wipe (CoDD)** and keep the certificate with your POPIA records.
