## Why this matters (safety + privacy)

- **Fire risk:** Damaged or loose lithium cells can short and ignite in bins or vans.  
- **POPIA risk:** Old drives contain IDs, client files, coursework. Deleting files is **not** a compliant wipe.  
- **Audit trail:** A **CoDD** (Certificate of Data Destruction) proves what happened to your data.

## Safe prep (visible steps mirror the HowTo)

1. **Power down.** If a battery is **swollen**, do **not** charge or puncture it.  
2. **Tape terminals** and bag each cell/device separately.  
3. **Back up** needed files; then authorise a **certified wipe** with CoDD.  
4. **Remove accessories** you intend to keep (SIM/SD/caddies/docks).  
5. **Label & log.** For businesses, we provide an intake log (serials, condition, handler).

## What your CoDD includes (audit-ready)

- Device identifiers (serials/drive IDs)  
- Wipe/destruction method (e.g., crypto-erase, pattern wipe)  
- Tool/version, **verifier**, date/time  
- Disposition (reuse/recycle/destroy) and your reference number

Keep it with your **POPIA** and **CPA** records.
