## Symptoms you’ll recognise (and likely causes)

- **Fans roaring constantly** → Dust-clogged fins, old paste, aggressive background load.  
- **Sudden shutdowns under load** → CPU/GPU temps spiking; paste dried out; obstructed intake.  
- **High idle temps** → Dust on heatsink or wrong fan curve; background miners/adware.  
- **One hot corner on the chassis** → Localised heatpipe/VRM contact issue or pad misalignment.  
- **GPU stutter after a few matches** → Thermal throttling; dried pads on VRAM; poor case airflow.

---

## Safe steps before you book (quick DIY)

1. **Surface clean only** (no tear-down): blow dust **from the outside** using short, controlled bursts; hold fans still to avoid overspin.  
2. **Check vents**: don’t block bottom/side intakes with bedding; elevate the rear slightly.  
3. **Update & monitor**: install OS/GPU updates; use a trusted temp monitor to get **idle/load** numbers.  
4. **Stop if you see swelling or burnt smells**: book professional service—safety first.

> Re-pasting thin-and-light laptops and many gaming rigs is **not** a beginner job—screws, cables, and pads are easy to damage. We handle the tear-down and **rebuild to spec**.

## Common pitfalls (and the fix)

- **Over-tightening heatsinks** → Warped contact = worse temps. We torque evenly in cross-pattern.  
- **Random paste types** → Wrong viscosity or conductive paste near SMDs = risk. We use **device-appropriate** compounds.  
- **Forgetting VRAM/VRM pads** → Gaps kill stability. We measure and **match thickness**.  
- **“Auto” fan curves only** → We tune curves for quiet **and** safe temps.  
- **Re-using broken clips/screws** → We replace or secure properly to prevent rattle and hot spots.
