## Common pitfalls (and the fix)

- **ISP router in the wrong spot** → Move/router-bridge + add APs in the right rooms.  
- **Mesh on auto-everything** → Lock **2.4 GHz** to a clean channel and set 5 GHz backhaul sensibly.  
- **“Range extenders”** → Replace with proper APs/mesh; extenders halve throughput.  
- **Printer/POS on guest SSID** → Isolate guests; reserve static IP for printers on staff SSID.  
- **Thick walls** → Use extra APs or partial wired backhaul rather than cranking transmit power.

## FAQs

**Q: Do I need mesh or will one router do?**  
**A:** If you struggle beyond 1–2 rooms or across floors, mesh or extra APs are the fix. We’ll place nodes and plan backhaul so roaming stays smooth.

**Q: Can you work with my ISP-supplied router?**  
**A:** Yes. We’ll bridge it (where supported) or tune it, then add APs/mesh to extend coverage properly.

**Q: How long does a typical setup take?**  
**A:** Most homes are 60–120 minutes depending on rooms/walls. Larger houses/guesthouses may need a site survey and a short follow-up visit.

**Q: Is on-site work safe and tidy?**  
**A:** Yes—our installs follow **on-site safety** practices (ladders, power, cable routing) and we label SSIDs/channels/PoE so future changes are easy.
