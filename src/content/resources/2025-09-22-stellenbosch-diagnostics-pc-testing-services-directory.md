## What the diagnostic actually checks

- **Power & battery health** — charger output, battery wear, DC-in stability.  
- **Thermals & cooling** — fan response, dust buildup, paste/pad condition (visual), temperature under load.  
- **Memory & storage** — RAM tests; SMART/NVMe health; read/write stability; file-system integrity.  
- **Boot & OS** — update state, startup impact, driver faults, malware indicators.  
- **Ports & peripherals** — USB-C/HDMI behavior, Wi-Fi/Bluetooth, camera/mic basics.

> For deeper faults (liquid damage/board issues), we’ll outline options and costs before proceeding.

1. **Original charger & any dongles** involved in the fault.

4. **Backups** if the device still powers on; or ask us to attempt a safe copy before tests.  
5. **Receipts** if warranty may apply (retail partners may need them).

## Do-nots (they hide the real fault)

- Don’t wipe/reinstall before a **backup attempt**—you may lose evidence and data.  
- Don’t run long stress tests on a **clicking** drive—escalate to data-safe checks first.  
- Don’t ignore **swollen batteries** or burning smells—shut down and bring it in safely.
