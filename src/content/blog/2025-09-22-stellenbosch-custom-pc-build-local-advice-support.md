## Identify your use-case (so parts fit the job)

- **Gaming (1080p/1440p/4K):** GPU tier and monitor refresh lead; CPU follows. Aim for 16–32 GB RAM and fast NVMe.  
- **Design/Adobe/DaVinci:** GPU VRAM + fast scratch SSD; color-quiet case and fan curves; 32–64 GB RAM.  
- **CAD/Engineering:** CPU instructions/single-thread, ECC where required, professional-grade GPU if the app benefits; 32–64+ GB RAM.  
- **Data/Dev:** cores + RAM capacity, quiet cooling for long tasks, fast NVMe with good TBW for build caches.

> We’ll map software to parts (and drivers) so you don’t overspend where it doesn’t help.

## Safe steps (our advisory → assembly workflow)

1) **Brief & compatibility**  
   - We capture your apps, monitors, ports, space, and noise limits. We check **socket**, **chipset**, **RAM speed**, and **GPU clearance**.

2) **Thermals & airflow plan**  
   - Choose a case with **front intake + rear/top exhaust**, dust filters, and room for cables. Positive pressure beats dust in Cape summers.

3) **Power budget**  
   - Size an **80+ Gold** PSU with **30–50% headroom** over typical load; modular cables reduce clutter and heat.

4) **ESD-safe assembly**  
   - Bench-test board/CPU/RAM first; apply **quality thermal paste**; route cables for unobstructed airflow; label front-panel and PSU leads.

5) **BIOS/UEFI & drivers**  
   - Update BIOS, enable **EXPO/XMP** (stable profile), set boot order, then clean-install OS. Apply chipset, GPU, storage controller drivers.

6) **Burn-in & validation**  
   - Run **CPU/GPU/memory stress**, game/creator benchmarks, and **thermal checks** at ambient. Verify idle temps, load temps, and noise.

7) **Delivery & docs**  
   - Provide a build sheet (serials, profiles, temps, benchmarks) and simple care tips. Optional **on-site** placement and cable routing.

## Do-nots (avoid expensive mistakes)

- Don’t pair **bargain-bin PSUs** with high-end GPUs. Voltage ripple kills stability.  
- Don’t mix **random RAM kits**; buy a matched kit for fewer QVL headaches.  
- Don’t choke airflow with glass-fronted cases and no intake.  
- Don’t skip **paste quality** or reuse factory pads outside spec.  
- Don’t run open-bench PCs in dusty rooms—fans become vacuums.

## When to upgrade vs rebuild

- **Upgrade GPU** if your CPU is recent and the workload is GPU-bound.  
- **Add RAM/NVMe** for creators who hit cache/scratch limits.  
- **Rebuild** if you’ve hit a platform ceiling (older socket/chipset) or thermals/noise can’t be fixed economically.
